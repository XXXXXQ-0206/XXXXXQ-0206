## About Me

I study in the Computer Science (Excellence Class) at Chongqing University. My interests center on systems that can perceive, remember, reason, and act in the physical world, with particular attention to grounded multimodal interaction and reliable autonomous behavior.

For personalized multimodal understanding, I am interested in connecting multiple visual references with retrieval, verification, temporal evidence, and memory so that an agent can reason from an evolving understanding of a person and their context.

I have also worked on SSVEP-based brain-computer interaction for humanoid assistance: decoding EEG signals into intent, applying incremental inverse kinematics with 50 Hz servo control, and driving dexterous-hand control. The system treats control as continuous intent prediction with confidence-gated confirmation, rather than a sequence of isolated commands.
